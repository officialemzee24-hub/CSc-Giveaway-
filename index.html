How it works

  1. 1

    Register

    Submit your name, matric number, email, phone, and level. One registration per matric number.

  2. 2

    Eligibility review

    The department reviews registrations and marks eligible students. This may take a few days.

  3. 3

    Submit account details

    Once marked eligible, return with your matric number to securely submit your account details.

  4. 4

    Processing

    The department processes submitted details. You will be notified through department channels.

Register

Registration closes automatically once the department's schedule ends or the maximum number of registrants is reached — whichever comes first.

  • Department defaults to Computer Science.
  • Each matric number may register once.
  • Registering does not guarantee eligibility.

Eligibility

Eligibility is reviewed by the department after registration closes or during the giveaway window. If you are marked eligible, the account details form linked below will accept your matric number.

Frequently asked questions

Who can register?

Any currently registered Computer Science student with a valid matric number.

Can I register more than once?

No. Each matric number can only be used for one registration.

What happens when all slots are filled?

Registration closes automatically and the form becomes unavailable, even before the closing date.

When will I know if I am eligible?

The department will announce eligibility through official department channels after review.

Why are account details requested?

Account details are required to process the giveaway payout to eligible students only.

What happens after I submit account details?

Your submission is recorded securely and moves to processing. It cannot be viewed or edited by other students.
